# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical founders, infrastructure engineers, agent-runtime authors, and design
partners evaluating whether celld creates a defensible substrate for durable
agents. They are reading critically, usually on a laptop, and need to separate
measured behavior from architecture proposals and product speculation.

## Product Purpose

Protein explains and implements the smallest useful agent primitive native to
celld: one named durable control-plane actor per independently addressable unit
of memory and authority. Success means a reader understands what an Agent Cell
owns, what remains external, which workloads create real deployment demand,
what was proven experimentally, and which production risks remain unresolved.

## Brand Personality

Skeptical, mechanical, decisive. The voice is intellectually honest and
evidence-led, with enough visual energy to make distributed-systems mechanics
legible without turning them into hype.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-agent landing pages, glowing chatbot imagery, orchestration-spaghetti
diagrams, terminal cosplay, glass dashboards, vague claims of autonomy, and
architecture reports that blur observation, inference, and aspiration.

## Design Principles

1. Label the epistemic status of every important claim.
2. Show the boundary before describing the feature set.
3. Make failure semantics as prominent as the happy path.
4. Use measured evidence to earn conclusions.
5. Prefer a sharp rejection criterion over a long possibility list.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, use semantic HTML, preserve keyboard navigation, never rely
on color alone, include text alternatives for diagrams, support zoom and narrow
viewports, and remove nonessential motion when reduced motion is requested.
